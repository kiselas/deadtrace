# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from feedback.
- Protect private information and avoid public speculation about security reports.
- Prioritize the health of the project and community over individual status.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an appropriate private
GitHub channel. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and
may remove content, issue warnings, impose temporary restrictions, or permanently exclude a
participant. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
